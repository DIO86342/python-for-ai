You now know:

Ridge: shrinks coefficients.
Lasso: shrinks coefficients and can make them exactly zero.
Alpha: controls regularization strength.
Too much alpha: causes underfitting.
Best alpha: should normally be selected using validation/cross-validation rather than arbitrarily.
