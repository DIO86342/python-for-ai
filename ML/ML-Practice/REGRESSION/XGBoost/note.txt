XGBoost = Extreme Gradient Boosting.

Gradient Boosting vs XGBoost

Think of ordinary Gradient Boosting as:

"Build trees sequentially to reduce the loss."

XGBoost goes further:

"Build trees sequentially while optimizing the objective efficiently and explicitly controlling model complexity."

One important difference is regularization.

Regularization helps prevent the model from becoming unnecessarily complex and overfitting the training data.
